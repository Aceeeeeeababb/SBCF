Get to know us

Faith is a journey best shared.

San Bartolome Christian Fellowship is a community learning to follow Jesus in everyday life. We gather to worship, grow through God’s Word, care for one another, and share hope with our neighbors.

Whether you’re exploring faith or looking for a church family, you are welcome to take your next step with us.

Our mission"To glorify God by obeying the great commission of making deciples that will make diciples from generation to generation"
